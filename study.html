Postgraduate Programmes

Master's Degree

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

  • 2 Years
  • Hybrid
  • 61 Credits

Master of Business Administration (MBA) program offered in collaboration with BOS on Management and Humanities is a UGC recognized SLQF Level 10 postgraduate degree. Our comprehensive curriculum blends theory with hands-on experience, guided by accomplished faculty.

Engage in industry partnerships, research, and experiential learning. Culminate your journey with a Final Viva Voce Examination, proving your mastery to a distinguished panel. Join us in preparing for a successful future in business leadership.

ProgrammeDurationModeResearch AreaEntry Requirement

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD)3–5 Years

  • Part-time – Five (05) Years

  • Management, Entrepreneurship, and Organizational Studies
  • Marketing, Tourism and Service Management
  • MPhil Degree relevant to the Subject
  • Four-year Bachelor’s (Special) Degree relevant to the subject with a First Class or Second Class (Upper Division) or Second Class (Lower Division)
  • Bachelor’s (General) Degree relevant to the Subject and a two-year Master’s Degree with a research component in the relevant field
  • Any other qualifications acceptable to the Senate

Entry Requirements – Postgraduate Programmes

  • Relevant first degree from a recognised university (minimum Second Class Lower for MBA)
  • Minimum 2 years of work experience preferred for MBA programmes
  • MPhil: Relevant first degree with Second Class Upper or above
  • PhD: Completed MPhil or equivalent research master's degree
  • English language proficiency — minimum IELTS 5.5 or equivalent
  • Two academic or professional references required with application

Level 4

Certificate Programmes

Certificate Course in English for School Leavers

Duration: 6 Months  |  Mode: Part-time (Weekends)

Certificate Course in English for School Leavers is a practical, short-term programme designed to equip recent school leavers with essential English language skills for higher education, employment, and everyday communication.

The course focuses on building confidence and competence in the four core language skills namely listening, speaking, reading, and writing, while also strengthening grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation.
