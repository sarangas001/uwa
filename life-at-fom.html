TC

UWU Tourism Club

Hospitality, Tourism & Events

The UWU Tourism Club, affiliated with the Department of Tourism Studies, Faculty of Management, Uva Wellassa University, provides a dynamic platform for students to enhance their knowledge, skills, and practical exposure in the tourism and hospitality industry. The Club promotes professional development, creativity, leadership, teamwork, and industry engagement through academic, cultural, social, and recreational activities.

It organizes and supports activities such as tourism awareness programmes, workshops, competitions, community-based initiatives, industry interactions, and tourism-related events. Through these initiatives, the Club encourages students to apply classroom knowledge to real-world contexts, develop professional networks, and actively contribute to the sustainable development and promotion of Sri Lanka's tourism sector.

Est. 2017 · 400+ MembersLearn more about UWU Tourism Club (opens in a new tab)

Campus Infrastructure

Facilities

FOM provides modern, well-maintained facilities to support academic study, collaborative work, student wellbeing, and campus life.

Housing & Dining

Comfortable student accommodation and convenient dining facilities designed to support a welcoming, comfortable, and engaging campus experience.

  • Student hostel accommodation for eligible students
  • On-campus dining facilities with affordable meal options
  • Common areas and recreational spaces
  • Facilities supporting student comfort and wellbeing
  • 24-hour security and maintenance

Laboratories

Modern learning equipped with computer labs that support practical coursework, software-based learning, data analysis, and academic research.

  • Computer laboratories with internet access
  • Laboratories for Tourism, Hospitality and Events Management
  • Business and data analysis learning facilities
  • Specialised facilities supporting practical and applied learning

Study Areas

Dedicated spaces for individual study, collaborative learning, reading, and academic engagement across the Faculty and campus.

  • Reading and resource facilities
  • Spaces for group discussions and collaborative work
  • Quiet areas for individual study
  • Access to digital and learning resources

General Campus Facilities

A range of shared facilities and support services that enhance the academic, social, recreational, and everyday campus experience.

  • Main Lecture Theatre and auditorium for academic and university events
  • Administrative and student support services
  • Sports and recreational facilities
  • Spaces supporting student wellbeing and community activities
