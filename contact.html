Ask From the Dean

About the Dean's Office

The Dean leads the Faculty of Management, with the Dean’s Office serving as the central administrative and governance hub for all academic operations, faculty management, student welfare, and strategic initiatives. Department Heads directly report to the Dean to maintain cohesive leadership across every academic department.

Beyond internal coordination, the office acts as the primary point of contact for students, staff, and external stakeholders requiring official correspondence, academic guidance, or the escalation of critical concerns.

Academic Affairs

Coordination of academic programmes, curriculum matters, programme approvals, academic policies, and decisions of Faculty and relevant academic boards.

Student Welfare

Addressing student concerns and grievances, supporting student welfare, coordinating scholarship-related matters, and handling disciplinary and other student-related affairs.

External Relations

Facilitating industry partnerships, institutional collaborations, MOU-related matters, research and professional engagements, and communication with external stakeholders.

Administrative Services

Supporting official correspondence, staff coordination, document certification, faculty-level approvals, records, and Faculty-wide communications.

How to Get Here

  • By Bus

    Direct bus services available from Badulla town to Uva Wellassa University. Bus Routes from Badulla to Passara stop at the university main gate. Journey: approx. 10–15 minutes from Badulla town.

  • By Train

    From Badulla Railway Station: Taxis and three-wheelers are readily available for travel to the Uva Wellassa University campus. The journey from the railway station to the Faculty of Management takes approximately 15–20 minutes, depending on traffic conditions.

  • By Car / Taxi

    The Uva Wellassa University campus is located approximately 5 km from Badulla town centre, with convenient access from the main entrance. Ample parking is available on campus. Upon arrival, follow the directional signage to the Faculty of Management building.

  • From Colombo

    From Colombo to Badulla: Travel by car via the expressway and Route 99, with the journey taking approximately 5–6 hours. Alternatively, trains from Colombo Fort to Badulla take approximately 10–12 hours, offering a scenic journey through Sri Lanka’s hill country.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common enquiries about programmes, applications, and campus life.

What academic departments fall under the Faculty of Management at Uva Wellassa University (UWU)?

The Faculty of Management comprises four main academic departments:

  • Department of Management Sciences
  • Department of Public Administration
  • Department of Tourism Studies
  • Department of English Language Teaching
What undergraduate degree programs are offered by the Faculty of Management?

The faculty offers four primary specialized degree programs:

  • Bachelor of Business Management (BBM) Honours in Entrepreneurship and Management
  • Bachelor of Business Management (BBM) Honours in Hospitality, Tourism, and Events Management
  • Bachelor of Business Management (BBM) Honours in Human Resource Development
  • Bachelor of Arts (BA) Honours in English Language & Applied Linguistics
What is the medium of instruction for all degree programs in the faculty?

All lectures, course materials, continuous assessments, and final examinations across the faculty are conducted in English.

How are students selected and admitted to the Faculty of Management?

Undergraduate selection is managed centrally by the University Grants Commission (UGC) of Sri Lanka based on G.C.E. Advanced Level results (typically from the Commerce stream) and z-scores.

Is there an orientation program for incoming first-year students?

Yes. Entrants undergo an intensive Orientation Programme, which includes foundational training in English and Information Technology (IT) to help transition to university-level academics.

What is the grading and evaluation structure for course units?

Courses generally follow the Grade Point Average (GPA) system, where final marks consist of:

  • 40% Continuous Assessments (CA): Quizzes, assignments, mid-term tests, presentations, and field trips.
  • 60% End-Semester Examination: Final written theory and practical exams.
What is the significance of the Broad General Education (BGE) component?

Broad General Education is a compulsory curriculum component across UWU designed to develop soft skills, ethics, social awareness, and general knowledge alongside core business modules.

Does the Faculty of Management require mandatory practical training or internships?

Yes. Degree programs include a compulsory industrial training/internship period and an undergraduate research project during the final year to give students hands-on industry exposure.

What digital platforms are used by students for academic activities?
  • VLE / LMS: Virtual Learning Environment for accessing lecture slides, quizzes, and submitting coursework.
  • UWU Web Mail: Official university email provided to every student.
  • CBMIS: Cloud-Based Management Information System for tracking academic records and course registration.
Where is the UWU campus located?

The university is located about 2.5 km from Badulla town along the Passara Road in the Uva Province of Sri Lanka.

Are hostel facilities provided for Faculty of Management students?

Yes, university hostel accommodation is provided for eligible students (typically prioritized for first-year entrants and final-year students), subject to university selection guidelines.

What documents are required for the university enrollment process?

Newly selected students submit the official UGC/UWU enrollment forms, hostel application forms (if applying), declaration forms, and the official bank payment receipt for registration fees.

What career opportunities are available for graduates of the faculty?

Graduates work in executive and managerial roles across corporate finance, commercial banking, hotel and resort management, event planning, public sector administration, supply chain, and entrepreneurship.

How does the university support sports and extracurricular activities?

UWU provides facilities for various indoor and outdoor sports and hosts university-wide cultural and talent programs, such as Glamouro for first-year students.

Whom should I contact for administrative or admission queries?

You can contact the Senior Assistant Registrar (Student Affairs Division) at 055-2226622 or 055-3050886 during office hours, or refer to the official university portal (www.uwu.ac.lk).

Does the Faculty of Management offer postgraduate degree programs?

Yes. Through the Postgraduate Unit (PGU), students can pursue advanced degrees such as the Master of Business Administration (MBA), Master of Philosophy (MPhil), and Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in management-related disciplines.

What is the focus of the Department of English Language Teaching (DELT) within the Faculty?

DELT provides essential English language training across the university, delivering English for Academic Purposes (EAP) and professional communication modules designed to ensure all students master English as the medium of instruction.

Are students allowed to apply for the Faculty of Management from Arts or Science G.C.E. A/L streams?

Yes. While the majority of students enter from the Commerce stream, select degree programs welcome eligible applicants from Arts, Science, or Technology streams depending on annual UGC entry criteria.

How does the Faculty support student research and academic publications?

Final-year undergraduates complete an independent research study under faculty supervision, with opportunities to present findings at national and international research symposia, such as UWU’s International Research Conference (IRCUWU).

What student clubs and societies operate under the Faculty of Management?

Students can participate in various academic and cultural student bodies, including the Tourism and Hospitality Student Association, Entrepreneurship Student Society, and Accounting & Finance Society, which host industry workshops, field visits, and community outreach projects.

How do I select my area of specialization of the degree program?

Specialization courses are offered in the 300 and the 400 levels of the program. Based on the specialization choice and performance in the 100 and 200 levels courses, students shall be allowed to select a field of specialization. Students shall not be allowed to change the field of specialization once it is selected.

Do I need to be fluent in English?

BBM program is conducted in English medium and therefore you need to have substantial knowledge in English. During the orientation period, new entrants are offered Intensive English Course assigning them to different groups based on the performance at the Intensive English Course Exit Test. After the orientation programme, English language courses are offered during the degree programme to further improve the knowledge in English. If students can improve their English language proficiency before entering the University, it would be an added advantage for their academic works.

Where can I get my transcript of records?

A transcript to every student shall be issued by the Examination Division for each semester. At the end of the degree program, a Final Transcript shall be issued with above details plus Overall GPA and class (if applicable). An Authentic Transcript (for a semester or full degree program) can be requested from the Examination Division by paying stipulated fee.

When and Where can I see my marks and grades?

Regarding the continuous assessments, the Head of Department shall display raw marks of continuous assessments on the relevant Department notice board. In the case of final examination, the letter grade of the final results shall be displayed on the notice board by the relevant Head of Department.

If I fail in any course, what should be done by me?

If a student obtains a grade of E (i.e. fail) for a course in any semester, he/she shall retake the course when it is offered next time with the grade of the retake replacing the original grade (E). Only three retake attempts are allowed for any single course, and the maximum grade obtainable on a retake is “C”. Thus, a student can register only for one proper take and three retakes during their tenure.

If a student fails in a course in his/her final year in the University, the student still has to follow the entire course and fulfill all the requirements when it is offered next.

What are the acceptable reasons to extend the duration of completion of degree program?

Extensions are granted on acceptable medical reasons certified by the Chief Medical Officer of the University Health Center or the Medical Board. Students granted extensions on medical grounds shall be eligible for honors if he/she completes the degree program within five years.

A student must submit a medical certificate approved by the Chief Medical Officer of the University Health Center with the request for extension. The Faculty Board shall study all such requests and make recommendations to the Senate.

Extensions can be granted for up to two years maximum per request. (Extensions on child birth and maternity reasons shall be considered as medical reasons.)

Requests for extensions on non-medical grounds shall be considered by the Faculty Board and the Senate shall determine their acceptability, and if acceptable, whether or not the student is eligible for honors.
