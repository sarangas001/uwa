Department of Management Sciences

Head of Department: Prof. N.P.R.Deyshappriya

The Department of Management Sciences, Faculty of Management is the home of the Bachelor of Business Management (Honours) in Entrepreneurship and Management, nurturing innovative thinkers, entrepreneurial leaders and management professionals equipped to create value in a dynamic business world. Through excellence in teaching, research, industry engagement and experiential learning, DMS develops students' capabilities across entrepreneurship, strategic management, human resource management, marketing, finance, operations and supply chain management, preparing them to transform ideas into sustainable ventures and meaningful business solutions.

DTS

Department of Tourism Studies

Head of Department: Ms. C.P. Danthanarayana

The Department of Tourism Studies at the Faculty of Management, Uva Wellassa University, is dedicated to excellence in tourism education, research, and industry engagement. The Department offers innovative and industry-oriented programmes that equip students with the knowledge, skills, and competencies required for careers in hospitality, tourism, and events management. With a strong emphasis on sustainability, entrepreneurship, digital transformation, and experiential learning, the Department promotes responsible tourism development while addressing emerging industry trends. Through academic excellence, research collaborations, field-based learning, events and strong partnerships with tourism stakeholders, the Department contributes to developing skilled professionals and advancing the sustainable tourism sector in Sri Lanka and beyond.

DPA

Department of Public Administration

Head of Department: Dr. M. Rubavathanan

The Department of Public Administration, Faculty of Management is the academic home of the Bachelor of Business Management (Honours) in Human Resource Development, fostering competent professionals, innovative thinkers and responsible leaders who are equipped to develop and manage human talent in today's dynamic organizational environment. Through excellence in teaching, research, public and industry engagement, and experiential learning, the Department equips students with knowledge and skills in human resource, organizational development, employee relations, talent management, performance management, training and development, strategic human resource planning and public administration. The programme prepares graduates to effectively lead and develop people, build productive and inclusive workplaces, and contribute to the sustainable growth and success of public, private and non-governmental organizations.

ELT

Department of English Language Teaching

Head of Department: To be announced

Recognizing the growing importance of English language proficiency and applied linguistic knowledge in contemporary academic and professional contexts, the Department of English Language Teaching (DELT) has initiated a full-time, four-year Honours Degree Programme in English Language and Applied Linguistics. The programme has been designed to provide undergraduates with a comprehensive understanding of English Language and Applied Linguistics while preparing them to apply their knowledge effectively in diverse professional and social contexts.

A central feature of the programme is the development of students' English language competencies, particularly in communication, advanced reading, and academic and professional writing. Alongside language proficiency, students are introduced to the scientific study of language and its practical applications, enabling them to understand linguistic phenomena and employ such knowledge in addressing real-world communication needs. The curriculum also provides opportunities for students to develop analytical and problem-solving abilities, IT literacy, presentation skills, and competencies related to organizing, planning, and implementing activities.

The programme extends beyond disciplinary knowledge by fostering a range of transferable and professional competencies. Through academic, practical, and research-oriented learning experiences, students are encouraged to develop research skills, reflective practices, leadership qualities, and decision-making capabilities. Particular attention is also given to sector-specific competencies, adaptability, and the cultivation of appropriate attitudes and mindsets required to respond confidently to changing workplace and societal demands.

Moreover, DELT seeks to create an educational environment in which students can transform their knowledge and skills into creativity, innovation, and entrepreneurial potential. The programme therefore encourages undergraduates to become independent thinkers, effective communicators, innovative problem-solvers, and adaptable professionals who are capable of identifying opportunities and responding constructively to emerging challenges. In doing so, the programme aims to empower a deserving and motivated group of undergraduates to pursue diverse career pathways while making a meaningful contribution to society.

The overall orientation of the programme is firmly grounded in the vision of Uva Wellassa University (UWU), “To be the Centre of Excellence for Value Addition to National Resources Base.” In parallel, its academic and professional objectives are directed towards fulfilling the University's mission, “To produce well-rounded, employable and entrepreneurial graduates equipped with knowledge, skills, values and attitudes to make an outstanding contribution to the national development whilst excelling in teaching, learning and research with a strong emphasis on value addition to the national resources.”

Thus, the Bachelor of Honours Degree Programme in English Language and Applied Linguistics is conceived as a holistic undergraduate programme that integrates disciplinary knowledge, language proficiency, professional competencies, entrepreneurial capabilities, and personal development. Ultimately, it aims to produce graduates who are not only academically competent but also employable, innovative, adaptable, reflective, and capable of assuming leadership and decision-making roles in their chosen fields while contributing effectively to national development.
